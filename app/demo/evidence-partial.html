Milestone 1 — grantee evidence

Grant: docs tooling · tranche 1 · submitted by the grantee

1. The code

The contract and the app live in a public repository: github.com/Baophan00/grant-tranche-genlayer. The contract source is at contracts/grant_tranche.py, the direct tests at tests/direct/test_grant_tranche.py, and the deploy and e2e scripts at scripts/. The repository is public and readable without an account.

2. The live app

The app is deployed at grant-tranche.surge.sh. It calls the contract on GenLayer Studio Network: opening a grant, submitting evidence and reading receipts are all contract calls, and every action shows the transaction while the consensus round runs.

3. What comes next

We are preparing the usage report. The plan is to count submissions and issued receipts from the contract history and publish the numbers with the next tranche.
