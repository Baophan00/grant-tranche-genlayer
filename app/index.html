Open a grant

The funder writes the grantee, the rubric and the tranche count once. The rubric cannot change afterwards: criteria that move after the work starts are not criteria.

Grants

A tranche is approved only when every criterion is met. Partial readings are kept: the best count for the current tranche stays, so a later, worse attempt cannot lower it.

What a round does

  1. Validators fetch the evidence page.
  2. For every criterion they answer met true or false and quote the fragment that supports it.
  3. The round settles only when the vector of true/false matches across validators; quotes and reasons are free.
  4. The contract counts the met criteria and issues the receipt when all are met.

Demo evidence

meets two of three criteria meets all three

What it is not

The contract holds no funds. It settles the question a payment depends on and writes an immutable receipt per tranche that a treasury or multisig executes.
