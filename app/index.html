What a round does

  1. Validators fetch the evidence page.
  2. Each criterion gets one true or false and the fragment that decided it.
  3. The round settles when the true/false vector matches; quotes are free.
  4. The contract issues the receipt only when every criterion is met.

What it is not

No funds are held. The contract settles the question a payment depends on and writes the receipt a treasury executes.
